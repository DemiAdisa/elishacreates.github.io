Hello!

I’m Elisha, a multimedia producer, marketing strategist, and stage manager based in Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island. I help organizations and creators bring their visions to life through media, live events, and marketing.

From digital series and live broadcasts to stage-managed galas and brand campaigns, I combine creative direction with meticulous organization. I studied Media and Communications at Birmingham City University in Birmingham, England and Marketing and Advertising Management at Holland College in Prince Edward Island Canada. My multicultural background, shaped by my professional experiences across the Caribbean, the UK, Latin America, and North America, enhances my collaboration, communication, and storytelling skills.

When we work together, you gain a partner who clarifies goals, develops a comprehensive plan, coordinates the team, and delivers polished results on time and within budget. If you have an idea, I’ll help turn it into a project that inspires and connects with audiences.
