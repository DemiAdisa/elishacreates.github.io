‘RomeIN Carnival’ is a four-episode web series hosted and conceptualized by Rome. This annual series celebrates Trinidad and Tobago's vibrant Carnival culture and the people who bring it to life.

Sponsored by telecommunications company Digicel, the series is distributed via YouTube, Facebook, and the company's official app.

For this project, my tasks included:

  • Developing each episode with Rome
  • Scriptwriting
  • Coordinating logistics with the crew and on-camera talent
  • Briefing the crew and on-camera talent for each episode
  • Location scouting
  • Supervising post-production
